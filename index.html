IntelliMed AI

Lead AI Engineer, Hawklogix · HIMSS26 Conference, Las Vegas, March 2026 · Live at NeuroHeart and Vascular Clinic, Knox Foot and Ankle, Cardiology Consultant and Hope Neurology

IntelliMed AI is a multi-agent platform that carries a patient visit from raw data to finished, billed clinical documentation. Five specialized agents work as one pipeline: Summar reads the chart, Penny writes the documentation, Clara checks the clinical logic, Bill codes the billing, and Lexi runs the administrative workflow around them, while MIRA, a separate AI receptionist, checks patients in at the front desk. It is in daily production at four US clinics, used by 9–10 physicians plus medical assistants and nurses.

Summarchart reader

Builds structured patient summaries on a LangGraph state graph with branching, retry loops, parallel execution and crash-resume state. Long charts are chunked by encounter, summarized in parallel on Claude Sonnet, merged, then verified. Three physician modes: a fixed 7-section default, the physician's own template, or a custom form PDF whose field names form an allow-list, with missing values omitted and never invented. Scanned PDFs pass a 4-engine OCR cascade.

PennyScribed

Writes structured SOAP notes from four parallel section calls (history, review of systems, assessment and plan, physical exam), merged and cleaned, plus surgical and radiology procedure notes and notes in the physician's own templates. The live conversation is the source of truth; undocumented fields are marked Not Mentioned and stripped from the final note.

Claraclinical intelligence

Contradiction alerts from old-versus-new history, medications, allergies and plan-versus-history checks, plus evidence-based recommendations from PubMed (top abstracts per diagnosis), each with its source link and a confidence score weighted 70% on evidence quality and 30% on quantity. Unsourced recommendations are dropped.

Billbilling automation

Exactly one CPT per visit, ICD-10 limited to diagnoses documented in the note and valid 2026 codes, and MIPS measures only when the insurer is Medicare or Medicaid. CPT goes to GPT first and ICD-10 and MIPS to Claude first, with cross-model fallback. Output is codes with a short reason each, no amounts and no claim submission.

Lexiadmin workflow

Inbound and outbound fax management with automatic physician-task generation and document routing, on an OCR service that classifies and extracts referral, lab, insurance and imaging documents. Also powers Magic Edit, which revises a note from a plain-English instruction, changing only what is asked and never inventing clinical facts.

Highlights

  • Sequential pipeline: Summar feeds Penny; Clara and Bill run in parallel; Lexi works semi-independently; MIRA runs independently at the front desk.
  • Five live EHR integrations, including OAuth token services and FHIR endpoints.
  • Specialty-aware generation with relevance verification, so output stays tied to the right clinical specialty.
  • Incremental summary merging that never drops earlier data when new documents arrive.
  • Billing errors cut by nearly half through automated ICD-10 and CPT assignment.
  • Production LLM controls: prompt caching on all prompts (1-hour TTL) with a costing dashboard that reports cost with and without caching.
  • 4-layer prompt-injection shield on pasted text: normalization, pattern detection and MiniLM similarity against about 90 known attack examples.
  • Guardrails: a 40k input-token limit, generate only from the source text, dates, numbers and medications copied exactly, and a verification step that checks claims against the source.
  • Delivery: Docker on Azure App Service, new versions tested in a staging slot then swapped, image tags in Azure Container Registry for rollback, CI/CD on every project.
  • Monitoring: call-ID logging with no patient information in logs, Log Analytics and Application Insights, health-check auto-restart and Azure Monitor alerts by email and Teams to the on-call engineer.
  • In development: a Prior Auth AI Agent for NeuroHeart.

Voice Audit AI

Designed, built and led end to end · In daily use in the USA · Client: TeleSpecialists, US telehealth provider

Voice Audit AI replaced a fully manual call-review process at a US telehealth provider. Reviewers once spent 25 to 45 minutes auditing a single care call. The system now fetches recordings from Five9 Cloud and RingCentral, then transcribes, scores and audits about 300 to 400 calls a day against clinical KPIs in 1 to 2 minutes each, checking what was said on the call against what was entered into the system. Manual audit was discontinued after it went live.

25–45 → 1–2 minaudit time per call
~80%QA efficiency gain, about 6x throughput
+15%customer satisfaction
300–400calls audited a day
9case-type workflows, each with its own KPI matrix
High 80s–low 90saccuracy of the tuned speech pipeline on test sets

Highlights

  • Recordings fetched from Five9 Cloud and RingCentral with Azure Blob backup, transcribed by Deepgram Nova-2 with speaker diarization as the primary engine (diarization, word-level timestamps and filler words built in, fast on telephony audio) and OpenAI Whisper as automatic fallback.
  • GPT-based correction layer for medical terminology in transcripts.
  • Hybrid scoring in three layers: rule-based fuzzy and regex matching, spaCy NER with escalation detection, and LLM scoring (GPT-3.5, Claude) across seven KPIs: greeting, response-time compliance, gather and verify, empathy, interruption handling, professionalism and repeat-back.
  • Rules alone could not handle phrasing variation, so ML and LLM layers were added; free-text LLM output was inconsistent, so scoring is structured JSON validated with Pydantic.
  • Custom-trained models: a RoBERTa case-type classifier (roberta-base, full fine-tuning, macro-F1 0.85–0.92) and Llama 3.1 8B with QLoRA on a single 24GB GPU (macro-F1 0.88–0.94), which made output consistent and ended API cost while keeping data in-company.
  • Empathy and professionalism scoring adds SetFit over transcripts fused with a wav2vec 2.0 speech-emotion model.
  • TeleCare cross-validation compares what agents asked on calls against what they entered in the system, catching data-entry mismatches manual audits miss, with automatic escalation detection for high-risk calls.
  • Auto-generated Excel and JSON audit reports are posted to TeleCare via API.

Intelligent EMR Data RAG Chatbot

IntelliMed EMR chatbot · Designed and built end to end · RAG for physicians

A conversational layer over the electronic medical record. Physicians ask questions in plain language and get grounded answers pulled from the patient's own record across DrChrono, Azalea and Athenahealth, instead of clicking through charts.

14Qdrant collections on Azure
70–170patients indexed a day
>90%less physician data-retrieval time (design target)

Highlights

  • Records chunked into 14 Qdrant collections on Azure with SapBERT (PubMedBERT) embeddings, which map medical synonyms and abbreviations (for example MI and heart attack) close together where general embeddings miss them.
  • An intent classifier (MiniLM embeddings with logistic regression) routes each query to the right collection group.
  • Hybrid dense and keyword retrieval with cross-encoder reranking, blended as rerank 50%, hybrid 35% and dense 15%, with question decomposition for complex queries.
  • Few-shot grounded generation on Groq Llama 3.1 8B Instant: answers come only from retrieved records, otherwise the system says the information is not available in the patient records.
  • Automatic data purge after 48 hours for privacy.

QPS: Quality Performance Scoring

Built end to end · Post-stroke follow-up calls (90-day check-ins) · Client: TeleSpecialists

The automated audit for post-stroke follow-up calls. Recordings are pulled from Azure Blob, transcribed and scored out of 100, and each patient's Modified Rankin Scale score is derived from what the nurse asked and what the patient answered.

100-pt7-parameter quality score per call
MRS 0–6Modified Rankin Scale scored per patient
90-daypost-stroke follow-up check-ins

Highlights

  • Seven scored parameters: opening, empathy, interruption handling, clarity, questions answered, patient satisfaction and closing.
  • Each patient's MRS score combines rule-based matching of nurse questions with LLM scoring.
  • Deepgram Nova-2 transcription with OpenAI Whisper fallback; the full report is posted back via API. Call volume varies with how many patients are due a follow-up.

PSR Insurance Intelligence

Built end to end · RCM platform · Client: TeleSpecialists · 40–60 calls a day

The AI audit system for outbound insurance-verification calls on the TeleSpecialists RCM platform. Every call is scored on a 13-parameter scorecard out of 130, and a six-point insurance evaluation matrix is tracked call by call.

130-pt13-parameter scorecard per call
7 outcomesauto-categorized by a fine-tuned Llama 3.1 8B
40–60insurance calls audited a day

Highlights

  • Six-point insurance evaluation matrix: insurance name, member ID, effective dates, card phone number, claims address or payor ID, and card availability, each tracked as asked, missed, replied or refused.
  • Calls are auto-categorized into 7 outcomes by a fine-tuned Llama 3.1 8B classifier trained with QLoRA.
  • Same speech and scoring foundation as Voice Audit AI: Deepgram transcription with diarization, structured scoring and validation.
